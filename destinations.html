Mountain 4.9
Jammu & Kashmir

Kashmir

Known as "Paradise on Earth," Kashmir captivates with the shimmering Dal Lake, vibrant Mughal gardens, and snow-laden meadows of Gulmarg. Autumn paints the chinar trees in brilliant shades of gold.

  • Best Time: April–June, September–November
  • Top Spots: Dal Lake, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg
  • Activities: Shikara rides, skiing, trekking

Beach 4.8
Goa, India

Goa

India's smallest state packs an enormous punch — white sand beaches, Portuguese colonial churches, vibrant seafood markets, and a festive spirit that never quite fades.

  • Best Time: November–February
  • Top Spots: Baga, Anjuna, Panjim, Old Goa Churches
  • Activities: Beach hopping, water sports, heritage walks

Adventure 4.8
Himachal Pradesh

Manali

Set in the Beas River valley, Manali is the launchpad for Himalayan adventures. From Rohtang Pass to the orchards of Old Manali, this hill station rewards every kind of explorer.

  • Best Time: October–June (avoid monsoon)
  • Top Spots: Rohtang Pass, Solang Valley, Old Manali
  • Activities: Paragliding, river rafting, skiing

Heritage 4.7
Rajasthan

Jaipur

The Pink City is Rajasthan's crown jewel. Explore Amber Fort, wander through the Johari Bazaar, and lose yourself in the grandeur of the City Palace complex.

  • Best Time: October–March
  • Top Spots: Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, City Palace, Jantar Mantar
  • Activities: Elephant rides, shopping, cultural shows

Nature 4.9
Kerala, India

Kerala

Glide through mirror-still backwaters on a wooden houseboat, trek through Munnar's tea gardens, or unwind with an authentic Ayurvedic treatment. Kerala restores the soul.

  • Best Time: September–March
  • Top Spots: Alleppey, Munnar, Wayanad, Kovalam Beach
  • Activities: Houseboat cruises, Ayurveda, wildlife safaris

Spiritual 5.0
Ladakh, India

Ladakh

The Land of High Passes sits at the top of the world. Its stark, awe-inspiring landscapes, Tibetan-Buddhist monasteries, and the electric blue Pangong Lake make it unlike any other destination on earth.

  • Best Time: May–September
  • Top Spots: Pangong Lake, Nubra Valley, Hemis Monastery, Leh Palace
  • Activities: Bike tours, trekking, monastery visits
